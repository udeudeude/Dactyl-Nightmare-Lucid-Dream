Measurement discipline
Use multiple camera viewpoints, identify hard shot cuts, and record image crop and camera assumptions separately. Coordinates are normalized to the original image dimensions, not the displayed size. The dataset does not itself determine physical scale, camera pose, or platform dimensions. Do not treat uncalibrated pixel lengths as world-space ratios.

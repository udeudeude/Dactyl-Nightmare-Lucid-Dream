# Original Dactyl Nightmare: opening, on-screen display, endings and operator interface

Research audit: **2026-11-13**. Scope: the **Virtuality 1000CS** game originally sold from 1991, not `Dactyl Nightmare 2`, `Dactyl Nightmare SP` on the 2000 series, or modern `Polygon Nightmare`. Dates refer to the source or **observed play**, not an assumed 1991 launch-build revision.

**Method boundary:** New external print documents and indexed original-hardware stills were reviewed in this audit; the 1991/92 GamePro video's **actual frame bytes could not be retrieved in this session** (Archive and YouTube streams inaccessible from the research environment). The time-coded GamePro observations below are explicitly **carried forward** from the verified video download and prior frame inspection documented in [PERIOD_VISUAL_TARGET.md](PERIOD_VISUAL_TARGET.md), not newly viewed frames. Preserve that distinction. No unsupported screens, art, photos, or video are imported into the application.

### An internally consistent 21-second countdown

In the previously logged GamePro V03 observations, the archive-time difference
between 791 s and 812 s is **21 seconds**. The gameplay clock decreases from
**0:47 to 0:26**, also **21 seconds**, while both player scores increase.
This is an unusually useful *internal consistency check*: the numerals behave
as a live gameplay countdown, not static promotional text. It does **not**
establish that every intermediate television frame was continuous, that the
two readings were captured with exact frame precision, or that any `0:00`
result screen was seen. A frame-extraction follow-up should confirm both
readings against source pixels and test intermediate timestamps.

## 3. Major newly discovered gameplay contradictions

**September 1992, Rajesh Goel, *Amiga World* vol. 8 no. 9, "Head to Ed: Virtually Amiga."** Firsthand visit to two units at the Limelight Club in New York. The author states explicitly that **two pterodactyls circle overhead** and that bird carrying/dropping produces the game's **only third-person viewpoint**, unlike ordinary first-person combat. The piece describes attack after firing too quickly or too many times without hits. This is important conflicting, period eyewitness evidence: our current network room simulates **one** bird, uses a **first-person** carry camera, and counts ten accepted shots. Do not silently force two birds across every revision; confirm from independent period footage and revision/version information. Original PDF: https://amigaland.com/dataz/press_magazine/Amiga_World/PDF_TXT/Amiga_World_Vol_08_09_1992_Sep.pdf (article extracted around lines 1652–1695 in indexed text).

**Manufacturer service manual (1993).** Bird after `b<n>` shots defaults to ten, but the manual **does not distinguish hits from misses**, whereas Goel's 1992 description suggests misses or rate might matter. This is unresolved. `d` allows the operator to force a bird attack, not the player.

**Original 1993 sales description.** Arcade History reproduces a description of the simpler `Shootout`/training mode and advanced `Capture the Flag` mode with axes, shields and crossbows. This is consistent with manufacturer F7/F8 selection. It is **not a title-screen image** and its historical date/provenance need verification before treating it as launch-build specification. https://www.arcade-history.com/game/12493/

**Independent contemporary music note.** Green's January 1995 fieldwork (printed p. 56) reports continuous three-dimensional sound and comparatively gentle electronic background music alongside weapons and creature cries. Our audio remains synthesized and approximate; avoid claiming original soundtrack recreation.

## 4. Other source leads for authentic transitions, title or score screens

1. **Virtuality original marketing material**, `Virtuality (1993) p.13`, reproduced as Green thesis fig. 3-2. Source is advertising text/illustration, distinct from in-game title. Her thesis fig. 3-3 reproduces a **participant-drawn top-down arena map**, which may independently help geometry reconstruction. Full PDF: https://ir.canterbury.ac.nz/bitstream/10092/4592/1/green_thesis.pdf
2. **GamePro V03 787–818 s** and extra footage around that section, particularly before and after the excerpt. Need unedited match boundaries rather than a TV-show montage.
3. **`Synthetic Pleasures` (1995)** production credits explicitly list original Dactyl Nightmare footage supplied by **Virtuality Entertainment and Horizon Entertainment**, a potentially independent footage source. https://culturesofresistancefilms.com/synthetic-pleasures-credits/ No specific title/results frame has yet been verified from this film.
4. **GamesMaster** era television challenges sometimes included original Virtuality pods, but no specific Dactyl match-ending screen was verified here. Avoid assuming a television graphics overlay is the game's own interface.
5. **2022 ctrl-alt-rees** and **2016 Kotaku** hardware sessions (V01, V02), where continuous playback may catch credit/start and end. Verify that an observed overlay belongs to the pod's game feed, not the recording's editor or operator Amiga terminal.

## 5. Implementation boundaries / recommended next work

**Evidence-backed now:** a real timed scoreboard/HUD with player scores, an optional period-style aerial arrival before player control, and a simple winning player determination at time expiry. **Uncertain presentation:** original HUD glyph shapes and precise positions (requires 791/812 source frames), original opening camera speed/trajectory, exact words/screens shown at zero, and whether any player-accessible settings/menu existed.

- Keep the existing modern iPhone menu usable and label it a *modern control shell*. Do **not** impose an imagined authentic title or `GAME OVER` screen over the game.
- Preserve a clear **operator vs player** distinction when adding historically inspired game modes.
- Research the **1992 two-bird account and third-person grab view** before changing authoritative room count or camera behavior. Ideally secure 1992 continuous source video showing the grab camera and both birds.
- Do not collapse `Dactyl Nightmare` (1000CS), `Dactyl Nightmare 2`, `Dactyl Nightmare SP` or modern `Polygon Nightmare` into one visual/gameplay reference set.
- If an authentic frame is acquired, annotate **source, timestamp, resolution, screenshot cropping, colors, score positions, observed language**, and whether it is `game`, `operator`, `television overlay`, `marketing` or `unknown`. Do not infer historical exactness from unlabeled social-media crops.

# Historical authenticity contract

The target is the original 1991 Virtuality 1000CS Dactyl Nightmare experience, not a modernized virtual-reality game. Preserve the original's idiosyncrasies even where current design conventions would discourage them.

## Rules for development

1. **Original behavior takes priority over comfort or convention.** Do not silently remove camera translation, low frame cadence, abrupt viewpoint changes, unusual controls, harsh sounds, arcade timing, or other documented behavior merely because it conflicts with 2026 design expectations.
2. **Evidence outranks aesthetic preference.** Distinguish authenticated 1000CS evidence from later SU2000 SP assets, fan reconstructions, contemporary eyewitness accounts, and engineering guesses. A plausible imitation is not a verified historical fact.
3. **Do not invent exactness.** Preserve provisional status for the three-second arrival, bird counts and attacks, avatar models, arena dimensions, and unrecovered title/results screens until evidence resolves them.
4. **Keep modern scaffolding separate.** Pairing, permissions, server connection, lens setup and troubleshooting can live in the phone's out-of-game interface. They should not be mistaken for original arcade visuals.
5. **Modern accessibility options must be opt-outs, not silent replacements.** Where historical motion or cadence is supported, default to the period-informed behavior, label uncertain details, and permit alternatives without claiming they are original.
6. **Validate against source material.** Every major visual or gameplay change needs a reference to dated original footage, a primary manual, an authentic asset, or a clearly labeled inference. When sources conflict, preserve the conflict.
7. **Do not publish proprietary recovered game assets.** Record reproducible measurements and build independently authored models unless rights are established.

## Known evidence and limits

- 1993 operator manual: 180-second default match, configurable ten-shot bird threshold, mode and operator controls.
- 1994–95 eyewitnesses: overhead/approach opening; exact path and duration unknown.
- 1992 eyewitness: two birds and third-person bird capture, in tension with later single-bird observations and current implementation.
- Original 1994 SU2000 SP disc: authentic later-series meshes and sounds, not verified 1991 assets.
- Period footage: game-specific HUD, platforms, characters and colors require frame-grounded reconstruction.

See [historical evidence](HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE.md), [opening research](ORIGINAL_STARTUP_AND_SCREEN_EVIDENCE.md), and [disc forensics](SU2000_DISC_FORENSICS.md).

## Acceptance standard

The user should be able to play a complete multiplayer match in Cardboard and recognize the original game's look, sound, pacing, perspective and behavior from memory. This is a **target**, not a currently achieved or objectively guaranteed claim. Hardware acceptance testing and source comparisons remain mandatory.

Reconstruction rules

The CS 1000 service manual documents ten shots by default. Per-player counting and the flight/capture sequence remain approximate. Changing this setting resets your shot count.

Display

Historical mode repeats each rendered frame for approximately 50 milliseconds. The original arcade frame rate was approximate.

Controls

Phone: left circle moves, drag right to look, FIRE shoots. Cardboard: head tracking and Bluetooth gamepad recommended. Landscape orientation required. Allow motion permission when prompted.

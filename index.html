DACTYL NIGHTMARE

Lucid Dream · experimental historical reconstruction · v0.1.1

Five checkerboard platforms, polygonal arena, projectile combat and flying pterodactyl. Geometry and physics are provisional, not a verified replica of the 1991 Virtuality game.

Multiplayer (experimental)

Share a room code with up to three friends. Requires internet; peer-to-peer connectivity is not guaranteed.

For an app without Safari bars: Safari Share → Add to Home Screen → Add, then launch from the new icon.

Controls

Phone: left circle moves, drag right to look, FIRE shoots. Cardboard: head tracking and Bluetooth gamepad recommended. Landscape orientation required. Allow motion permission when prompted. Keyboard: W A S D, arrows, Space.
